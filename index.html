🛠️ How to Install:

  1. Download & Unzip: Click the button above and extract the ZIP folder to your computer.
  2. Open Extensions Page: Depending on your browser, type this in your address bar:
    • Edge: edge://extensions
    • Chrome: chrome://extensions
    • Brave: brave://extensions
  3. Enable Developer Mode: Turn on the "Developer mode" toggle (usually in the top-right or bottom-left corner).
  4. Load Extension: Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the extracted folder (the one containing manifest.json).

Note: After installing, pin the extension to your toolbar for easy access!
